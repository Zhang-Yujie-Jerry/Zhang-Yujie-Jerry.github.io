Position Openings

We are seeking passionate and self-motivated Postdoctoral Fellows, Ph.D. Students, and Research Assistants. Multiple fully funded Ph.D. scholarships and postdoctoral fellowships are available.

We also welcome visiting students, scholars, professors, and interns. Due to the number of applications, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.

Ph.D. Application

Ph.D. candidates typically study for three years with a master’s degree or four years with a bachelor’s degree. Positions are supported by research grants. Applicants are encouraged to review the following funding opportunities:

  • Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship
  • NTU Research Scholarship
  • A*STAR Graduate Scholarship
  • Interdisciplinary Graduate Programme
  • Other NTU Scholarships

Interested applicants should apply through the NTU Graduate Application Portal and review the EEE Ph.D. Admission Requirements.

Email your CV, research interests, referee contact, and transcripts to yujie.zhang@ntu.edu.sg with subject “Ph.D. Application – Name – Affiliation”.

Postdoctoral Positions

We seek candidates with expertise in antennas, electromagnetics, RF or microwave circuits, or AI-driven electromagnetic design. Fellows typically work with us for two to three years.

Applicants may also consider the following fellowship programmes:

  • Lee Kuan Yew Postdoctoral Fellowship (LKYPDF)
  • Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship (PPF)
  • SG Academies South-East Asia Fellowship (ASEA Fellowship)
  • Research Assistant Professorship (RAP)

Email your CV, a one-to-three-page research statement, and three referee contacts to yujie.zhang@ntu.edu.sg.

Research Assistant

Research Assistants typically work with us for one to two years. Programming, data analysis, prototyping, or wireless-system experience is preferred.

Visiting Opportunities

Students, researchers, and professors are welcome to send a CV and brief visiting plan to yujie.zhang@ntu.edu.sg.
